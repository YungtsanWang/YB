About Us

YB Intelligent Technology (Taizhou) Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer and solution provider specializing in In-Mold Labeling (IML) automation.

We focus on the development and integration of IML robots, labeling mechanisms, and complete IML systems for injection molding manufacturers worldwide. With strong engineering capability and rich project experience, we help customers improve production efficiency, labeling stability, and automation performance.

From system design to on-site commissioning, YB Intelligent provides customized IML solutions tailored to different molds, products, and production requirements. Our equipment is widely applied in food packaging, daily-use containers, and industrial plastic products.

Driven by innovation, reliability, and long-term cooperation, YB Intelligent Technology is committed to becoming a trusted IML automation partner for customers around the world.
